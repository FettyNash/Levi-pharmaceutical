Who We Are

Your Trusted Healthcare Partner

LEVI Pharmaceutical Plc is an Ethiopian company focused on the importation, marketing, and distribution of pharmaceutical and healthcare products. We aim to connect healthcare providers with reliable products sourced from reputable manufacturers worldwide.

Our portfolio includes medicines, medical equipment, laboratory products, diagnostics, and healthcare consumables. We are committed to professionalism, quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction while supporting the development of Ethiopia's healthcare sector.

With a dedicated team and strong partnerships, we work to ensure that healthcare institutions, pharmacies, clinics, NGOs, and healthcare professionals have access to the products they need.
