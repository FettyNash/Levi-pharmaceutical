About LEVI Pharmaceutical

Your Trusted Partner in Healthcare

LEVI Pharmaceutical Plc is an Ethiopian company focused on the importation, marketing, and distribution of pharmaceutical and healthcare products.

We aim to connect healthcare providers with reliable products sourced from reputable manufacturers. Our portfolio includes medicines, medical equipment, laboratory products, diagnostics, and healthcare consumables.

We are committed to professionalism, quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction while supporting the development of Ethiopia's healthcare sector.
